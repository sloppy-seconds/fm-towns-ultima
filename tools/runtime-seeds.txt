# THE 35 FUNCTION STARTS THE RECOMPILER NEVER RESUMES AT, derived by tools/resumepoints.js.
#
# WHY THEY ARE MISSING THERE. Every one is the same shape: its function ends at a terminating `ret`, then come 2 to 4
# bytes of alignment padding (`8b c0`, `90`, `00 00`, `66 8b c0`), then a new function begins. The recompiler's walk is
# seed driven and these functions have no caller, so nothing ever resumes after the `ret`. This project's walk resumes
# because it does gap recovery after alignment padding. Measured: 138 call instructions that our walk decodes and its
# does not, 137 of them in that gap, across these 35 functions.
#
# THE EVIDENCE THAT THEY ARE FUNCTIONS. 25 of the 35 begin with `55 8b ec` (push ebp; mov ebp, esp) or `c8` (enter).
# Two of the rest begin `53 56 57`, the register-only prologue, and one is the thunk at 0x103d5c. What they are not is
# called directly by anything, which is why both the `E8` rule and the recompiler's seeds miss them.
#
# TWO WERE SUSPECT AND BOTH FAILED THE TEST, which is why they are not in the list below. 0x1019f6 decoded as
# `pop edi` followed by `65 78 69` ("_exi") and 0x101602 as a prefixed jump followed by `74 65 6c 6c` ("tell"), both of
# which spell text, so this project's walk had wandered into a string. Seeding them made the recompiler refuse at
# 0x101a1c (`ff /7`, an invalid opcode group) and report 59 conflicts clustered from 0x1019ff to 0x101a9e, which is the
# entry point's neighbourhood and not a coincidence. With them removed the recompile is 0 refused and 0 conflicts. The
# 33 that remain are the ones this file carries, and the two failures are recorded here rather than deleted.
